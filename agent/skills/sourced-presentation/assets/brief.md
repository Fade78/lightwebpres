# Brief prompts

Use this aid when a brief is missing or incomplete, not as a mandatory form.
Free-form instructions and existing records are valid: extract their meaning
and ask only for gaps that affect the work. Do not require duplicate answers.

## Distinguish field states

- **Absent:** not supplied; consult existing instructions before asking.
- **Unknown:** not yet known; identify what could resolve it.
- **Not applicable:** irrelevant to this task; do not demand a value.
- **Forbidden:** explicitly disallowed; never replace with a default.

Record defaults separately from supplied facts and permissions. For authorized
corrections to existing content, default to improvement within the named scope.
No default grants redesign, a thesis change or publication. Apply standing
permissions; do not treat an absent field as either permission or prohibition.

## Recover the decisions needed

- **Audience and language:** knowledge, register, target language, intended gain.
- **Questions and thesis:** reader's questions; proposed thesis or open question;
  what remains undecided and which value premises matter.
- **Intention:** explain/compare, review knowledge, argue, or a stated combination.
- **Action and output scope:** create, audit, propose or correct; card, page or
  series; long and short forms, variants, improvement or authorized redesign.
- **Corpus and dated state:** current text, recoverable baseline if revising,
  source packet, assessment dates, access limits, prior findings and open work.
- **Requirements and references:** applicable editorial instructions, evidence
  records, citation conventions and acceptance criteria; reuse existing links.
- **Permissions and tool contract:** authorized editing, research, rendering and
  delivery; standing restrictions; required format, tools and output checks.

Pause only decisions blocked by unresolved permission, thesis or necessary
access; continue independent work covered by the mandate.
