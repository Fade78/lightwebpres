# Evidence checks

Use for research or a changed claim, study, quantity, quotation, allegation
or guarantee. Apply relevant checks; do not impose an experimental protocol
on a legal document or a manufactured controversy on an exposition.

## Research and source access

- Define questions, eligibility and exclusions before selecting results.
  Record why scope changes and revisit earlier selections. Check that
  excluding overlap does not remove a question from every page.
- Reuse the existing source packet and its dated corrections. Reopen what
  a change invalidates; do not repeat every search because drafting resumed.
- Trace repeated claims to their origin without presuming them stale.
  An interface suggests a mechanism to investigate, not what users think
  or feel. Research may confirm, narrow, overturn or leave a belief unresolved.
- Record each claim separately: assessment, author, title, date, source
  location, publication status and actual access. Add design, population,
  comparison, measurement and derivation when relevant. Keep rejected and
  unresolved claims, not only publishable ones.
- Verify a commissioned synthesis's identities and claims at the sources.
  Then revisit the research questions: checking returned numbers cannot
  reveal an omitted question. A bibliography entry does not prove its sentence.
- Read the method and results for study-based claims. If access stops at
  an abstract, excerpt or later report, cite that level and restrict the
  claim accordingly. Never describe an inaccessible body as fully checked.
- Put unresolved source identity or wording in the published source note
  when material. Replace or omit a claim without adequate support. A failed
  search establishes its own boundary, not an absence in the world.

## Weight, disagreement and bias

- Assess design, relevance, precision and consistency for empirical claims;
  records and applicable texts for events or obligations. Peer review,
  prestige and credentials are statuses, not reliability verdicts.
- Examine replications and inconsistent findings for load-bearing results.
  Compare tasks, populations and outcomes. A well-matched failed replication
  can weaken the original; changed conditions can bound it. Do not protect
  the study merely because it supplied the opening.
- Include pertinent benefits, harms, null and inconclusive results under
  the same criteria. Space follows evidence, not equal-sized camps. A more
  recent date or more precise number does not automatically settle conflict.
- Record funding and relevant ties; explain any claim-specific bias risk.
  Neither conflict nor independence decides credibility. Failure to find
  a disclosure does not confirm absence of conflicts.
- Statements against interest or addressed to investors can be informative,
  but are not presumed candid. Check content, context and corroboration;
  private audiences can also receive persuasion.
- Reverse the desired conclusion as a consistency test. Would the same
  methods, incentives, silence or uncertainty be judged differently?
  The editorial mission selects questions, not admissible outcomes.
- Separate established facts, interpretations and value premises in an
  argument. Explain the inference and assumptions, including why a proposed
  consequence is desirable. Evidence of effectiveness alone does not settle
  acceptability. A position may be defended without equal airtime for every
  objection, but not by hiding decisive counterevidence.

## Status and quantities

- Keep filing, allegation, ruling, settlement and admission distinct.
  Attribute a vendor's promise or measurement unless independently verified.
  A response is evidence that it was made, not automatic proof of its content.
- Separate observation, association, experiment and causal interpretation.
  Identify assumptions and plausible competing explanations. A reported
  feeling is not physiological damage; self-assessment is not independently
  verified performance; an action is not necessarily its intended outcome.
- Give quantities their units and observation level: people or accounts,
  continuous or cumulative time, individual or group average, time per
  person or study duration. Keep the necessary population, window and comparator.
- Name what was measured, not a larger construct. A delay to the next action
  is not full cognitive recovery; time before returning can include other
  work. Results on exposure, reactions, intentions and acts are not interchangeable.
- Check numerator and denominator before calculating a proportion. Matching
  units alone are insufficient. Record the derivation and preserve exact
  meaningful thresholds; use justified approximation rather than false precision.
- Interpret effect estimate, uncertainty, design and practical importance
  together. Non-significance is not equivalence. Missing or imprecise data
  do not establish absence; precise evidence may support a bounded absence claim.
- Keep the kind of result and material limits in every layer that carries
  it. Detailed qualifications may live elsewhere; they cannot repair an
  overstated headline. Alarm and reassurance require the same evidential care.

## Actor documents, quotations and responses

- When examining an actor, read relevant materials for users and other
  audiences, including advertisers, investors or authorities. Different
  audiences do not establish contradiction; compare dated scope and wording.
- Trace a quotation to an identifiable original and read its context.
  Mark translations and check them against the original. Keep qualifications
  when excerpting; respect reuse limits in notes as well as bodies.
- Preserve dated wording or a suitable record, not only a mutable URL.
  Describe the record actually available. Public material is not secret or
  leaked merely because the intended reader has not seen it.
- Preserve pertinent responses, corrections and later evidence from any
  party. They can narrow or overturn the starting account. Do not invent
  rebuttals or stop solely to give a preferred party the last word.
- Silence is not admission. Apply this equally to an accusation and a
  response. An unanswered claim still needs evidence; an unlocated response
  is not proof that none exists.
- For a new allegation, seek the response within the authorized reporting
  workflow. Do not contact people without authorization or manufacture an
  exchange merely to report existing research.
- Read guarantees against the actor, data, operation and conditions covered.
  Not selling is not not holding; protection against one party is not
  protection against all. Preserve real safeguards without turning them universal.
- Date claims that can expire. When wording or scope changes, recheck the
  article and every dependent card, not just the reference URL.
