# Creation and revision workflow

Use before drafting, auditing, proposing or correcting an editorial pair.
For French content, read [French writing checks](french.md) automatically
before writing or judging any layer, including titles and summaries.

## Establish the mandate

Identify three independent choices:
- **Action:** audit reports findings; propose presents candidates; correct
  edits the authorized content. Discussion alone does not authorize editing.
- **Scope:** card, page or series, with the affected units named.
- **Mode for existing content:** improvement by default; redesign only with
  explicit authorization for a defined scope.

“Assess conformity” is an audit; “bring into conformity” authorizes corrections
within the named scope, in improvement mode unless redesign is requested.
Improvement repairs demonstrated defects while preserving successful material
and organization. It can include a creative rewrite of an unclear paragraph.
Redesign changes unit grouping, order, number or roles; redistributing an
argument across pages is redesign. Mode depends on the change, not its length.
Neither mode silently authorizes a new thesis or publication.

Read the brief, applicable requirements, corpus and current work state.
If the brief is missing or incomplete, use [brief prompts](../assets/brief.md)
to recover needed decisions; accept free-form answers and existing records.
Resolve uncertainty only where it affects the next decision.

## Create from a brief and corpus

For new content, establish audience, questions, intention, thesis or open
question, output scope and source access. Reuse the available corpus and
identify evidence gaps before researching. Establish the article's argument
before deriving cards, following [editorial explanation](explanation.md).
Apply the skill's gates and the canonical
[reading passes](verification.md#reading-and-verification-record).
There is no prior draft to preserve; do not manufacture a revision baseline.

## Preserve the actual starting point

For revision, read the real current text, not just a past completion report.
Keep a recoverable snapshot including uncommitted changes; a repository
revision alone is insufficient when the working text differs. Protect others'
work. Preserve existing data, derivations, tables and evidence records as well
as the prose. Reuse research and decisions rather than rebuilding them.

For each finding, record the applicable requirement, affected units, intended
repair, qualities to retain and checks needed. Keep the chain inspectable:
**findings → requirements → units → checks**, with decisions and status.
Confirm that a reported defect still exists and is more than a preference.
Defects include demonstrable gaps in explanation, idiom, interest, voice or
progression against the brief, not only factual errors. Preservation must not
freeze unclear or ineffective writing.

For redesign, keep the candidate separate from the baseline until comparison
supports replacement under the mandate. Track the new home of each displaced
contribution, or the reason for removal. Retaining its citation does not prove
that its explanation survived. If improvement requires structural redesign,
describe the defect and proposed scope, then request the missing authorization.

## Repair from established material

Reuse dated, traceable evidence without freezing the wording. Sources constrain
meaning and evidential strength, not the syntax of the target language.
Use [change triggers](verification.md#change-triggers) to determine what must
be reopened; do not restart research reflexively. Repair an affected defect
in the article before deriving the card correction. Preserve an article passage
that has no relevant defect; agreement between article and card is not proof.

Edit what repairs the finding, then reread the whole affected unit and its
connections. Check documentary fidelity and target-language quality separately,
then check that neither repair degrades the other. Use the canonical criteria
in [explanation](explanation.md) and [verification](verification.md), rather
than creating a competing checklist here.

## Compare before accepting

Compare before and after against the stated defect and the qualities to retain.
Accept a repair only when it resolves the defect without an identified
regression. Rework a loss of scope, reasoning or established quality, or retain
the baseline. Make unavoidable tradeoffs explicit for a decision; uncertainty
about replacing a successful passage warrants a proposal, not silent overwrite.

A model, session or skill change does not authorize corpus regeneration or
restoring older wording. Before generalizing work after a model change or an
extensive redesign, run a scoped pilot on representative units that test both
language and evidence preservation. Compare without author labels when feasible.
A successful pilot does not validate remaining units: retain their checks under
[verification](verification.md#reading-and-verification-record).

## Scale and hand off

Work in reviewable batches with a resumable record. A mandate covering a series
does not require renewed approval after every page or already authorized step.
Stop the affected work for unresolved permission, thesis conflicts or necessary
source access; continue independent authorized work when it remains valid.
Follow local tool and delivery permissions, including standing authorizations.
Do not invent a no-build policy; honor local restrictions and use
[tools and delivery checks](verification.md#tools-and-delivery).
Report preserved, improved, redesigned and blocked units, actual checks and
untested layers. Partial work is not complete conformity; simulated reading
and agreement between agents are not measured audience reception.
